## Topic spine
- **Pure:** algebra & functions, quadratics, polynomials, indices & surds, coordinate
  geometry, sequences & series, binomial expansion, trigonometry & identities,
  exponentials & logarithms, differentiation, integration, and their applications.
- **Statistics:** data representation & measures, probability, permutations &
  combinations, discrete random variables, the binomial distribution, the normal
  distribution, correlation & regression, sampling.

## Recurring skills
Algebraic manipulation; solving equations/inequalities; curve sketching; differentiate/
integrate and apply (rates, areas, stationary points); trig equation solving; log laws;
probability reasoning; choosing and applying the right distribution; interpreting a
statistical result in context.
