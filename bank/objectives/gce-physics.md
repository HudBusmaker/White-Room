## Topic spine (standard A-Level)
- Measurement & experimental physics: SI units, uncertainties, vectors.
- Mechanics: kinematics, Newton's laws, momentum, energy/work/power, circular motion,
  gravitation, simple harmonic motion.
- Matter: deformation of solids, pressure, thermal physics, ideal gases, kinetic theory.
- Waves: wave properties, superposition, interference, diffraction, stationary waves,
  sound, the electromagnetic spectrum.
- Electricity & magnetism: current/voltage/resistance, DC circuits, capacitance,
  electric fields, magnetic fields, electromagnetic induction, alternating current.
- Modern physics: photoelectric effect, wave-particle duality, atomic structure,
  nuclear physics, radioactivity.

## Recurring skills
Unit/dimension checking; resolving vectors; multi-step numerical problems; deriving from
first principles; reading and using graphs (gradient/area/intercept); uncertainty
propagation; applying a law to a novel scenario.

## Quest generation
- Weekday morning: 1 worked problem (full working) + 3–5 MCQ-style checks on one topic.
- Weekend: a mixed mock or a derivation-heavy structured block, marked per-step.
Pull weakest/overdue topic first.

## Marking
Award method marks; check units and significant figures; flag the exact step that failed;
require working, never bare answers.
