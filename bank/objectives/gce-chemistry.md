## Topic spine — the three branches
- **Physical chemistry:** atomic structure, bonding, moles & stoichiometry, energetics/
  enthalpy, kinetics, chemical equilibrium, acids & bases / pH, redox, electrochemistry,
  rates.
- **Inorganic chemistry:** periodicity, Group trends, the transition elements, qualitative
  analysis of ions, industrial processes.
- **Organic chemistry:** functional groups, nomenclature, isomerism, reaction mechanisms
  (substitution/addition/elimination), homologous series, polymers, synthesis routes.

## Recurring skills
Mole/stoichiometry calculations; balancing equations (incl. redox half-equations);
enthalpy/Hess-cycle calculations; equilibrium & Kc/Kp; pH calculations; drawing and
explaining organic mechanisms; predicting products; interpreting qualitative-analysis
observations.
