## Topic spine
- **Further Pure:** complex numbers, matrices & transformations, further algebra &
  series, further calculus, polar coordinates, hyperbolic functions, differential
  equations, proof by induction, roots of polynomials.
- **Further Statistics:** further discrete & continuous distributions (e.g. Poisson,
  geometric), hypothesis testing, chi-squared tests, further hypothesis/estimation,
  further correlation/regression.

## Shared-Pure link (important)
The Pure core overlaps heavily with Mathematics. Do NOT drill shared Pure topics twice.
When a shared Pure item is mastered in either subject, credit both. Read the shared_pure
tags in mastery/gce.json and treat a mastered shared item as satisfying both fronts.

## Recurring skills
Complex-number arithmetic & Argand reasoning; matrix operations & transformations;
proof by induction; solving differential equations; applying a further distribution;
running a hypothesis / chi-squared test end to end and stating the conclusion in context.

## Quest generation
- Weekday morning: one Further Pure or Further Stats problem, full working.
- Weekend: a timed structured block, marked per-step. Prefer topics that build on the
  week's Mathematics work so the two reinforce.

## Marking
Method marks; require rigorous proof structure where asked; check the conclusion is
stated in context for tests; name the failing step; require working.
